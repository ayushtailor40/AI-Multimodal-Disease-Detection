Dataset Integrity & Limitations:
  • Image-level vs Patient-level Split: For the Lung Cancer CT dataset, image-level partitioning was applied in the benchmark source. In clinical environments, patient-level separation is required to rule out anatomical correlation across slices.
  • Multimodal Clinical Fusion Data: The clinical vitals dataset contains controlled synthetic representations designed to demonstrate mathematical late fusion. In real-world deployment, electronic health records (EHR) require extensive imputation and prospective calibration.
